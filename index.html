The Impact of Your Gift

HCSA is a partner of the Akron-Canton Regional Foodbank, so every dollar helps us stock the pantry with staples, fresh produce, bread, and protein for neighbors facing job loss, illness, fire, or other hardship.

  • Money: donate online for the Food Pantry, Helping Hands, Holiday Lighting or the General Fund, or mail a check to HCSA, P.O. Box 1472, Hudson, OH 44236 (note the fund in the memo line).
  • Gift cards: let us buy the extra items our shelves need.
  • Food and personal items: a 24/7 donation bin is outside Rejoice Lutheran Church (7855 Stow Rd) and at Hudson Town Hall (27 E Main St). Please, no expired food.
  • Hosting a drive? Call 234-380-7402 to learn current needs.

HCSA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit; contributions are tax deductible.

Receiving Food

We are a full choice pantry: take the items you know you and your family can use, including produce, bread, frozen meat, dairy when available, and toiletries.

  • When: 4th Saturday of each month (except major holidays), 12–2 PM. It is usually less crowded 12:45–1:30 PM.
  • Where: Rejoice Lutheran Church, 7855 Stow Rd, Hudson.
  • First visit: bring a state ID and a piece of mail showing your address.
  • Check in with a volunteer, then choose your groceries.
  • Can't attend? Ask about a proxy form, an appointment, or emergency food.

Questions? Call 234-380-7402 and leave a message (please don't call the church), or email hcsapantryteam@gmail.com.

Volunteer Options

Volunteers make everything we do possible. Find a role that fits your schedule.

  • Food Pantry: check in neighbors, stock shelves, and help with distribution day.
  • Helping Hands: support neighbors in crisis with rent, utilities, clothing, and gas assistance and referrals.
  • Food drives: organize a collection for your school, church, or business.
  • Organizations: join HCSA and send a representative to the monthly meeting.
